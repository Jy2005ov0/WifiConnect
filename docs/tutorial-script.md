# WiFi Connect tutorial: narration script

A voice-over for `docs/WiFi-Connect-Tutorial.mp4` (about 70 seconds). Each part matches a scene in the video. Read it at a relaxed pace; the times are where each scene starts.

You can also use this as a plan for recording your own video: film each scene on your phone with its screen recorder (iPhone: Control Centre › Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings › Screen record) and read the line over it.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | WiFi Connect title | "Tired of typing your student ID every time you join utarwifi? WiFi Connect signs you in with one tap, on iPhone, iPad, Android and your Windows laptop." |
| 0:05 | Step 1: Download | "First, open the Latest build release on our GitHub page. No account needed. Download the APK for Android, or the IPA for iPhone and iPad." |
| 0:11 | Step 2: Install | "On Android, open the file and allow the install. On iPhone or iPad you don't need a Mac: use Sideloadly or AltStore on a Windows PC with your Apple ID. On a laptop, just double-click Install." |
| 0:19 | Step 3: Welcome page | "Open the app. You'll see the welcome page with the time. Swipe up to start." |
| 0:24 | Step 4: Settings | "Type your student ID and password, then tap Done. They're saved encrypted, only on your phone." |
| 0:29 | Step 5: Join utarwifi | "Now join utarwifi in your phone's Wi-Fi settings. If the login page pops up, you can just close it." |
| 0:34 | Step 6: Tap the circle | "Back in WiFi Connect, tap the big blue circle. The app finds your building's login page and signs you in for you." |
| 0:39 | Step 7: Connected | "When it turns green, you're online. It works in every block, and you can tap the green circle to sign out." |
| 0:44 | Step 8: Automatic | "Want it fully automatic? Android already signs in by itself when you join. On iPhone and iPad, tap Auto Sign-In and set up the Shortcut once." |
| 0:51 | Help | "Stuck? Tap the question mark at the top for a step-by-step guide." |
| 0:57 | More | "There's also a speed test, your sign-in history, and five languages: English, Malay, Chinese, Japanese and Tamil." |
| 1:03 | Get started | "That's it. Download it, add your student ID, and you're online in one tap." |

## Adding the voice-over

- **On a phone:** open the video in CapCut (free), tap **Audio › Record**, and read along.
- **On a computer:** Clipchamp (built into Windows 11) or iMovie: drop the video in, then record a voiceover track.
- Keep music quiet under the voice, and export at 1080p.
